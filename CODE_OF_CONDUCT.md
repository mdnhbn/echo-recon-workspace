# Community code of conduct

We want students, classmates and contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels to feel welcome in this project.

- Be respectful and patient. Explain unfamiliar terms and make space for beginner questions.
- Review ideas and code, not someone's identity, ability or character.
- Give useful, specific feedback and credit other people's work.
- Accept that maintainers may request changes or decline a proposal with an explanation.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or repeatedly insult other participants.
- Do not publish private messages, personal information, credentials or target evidence without permission.
- Do not use project discussions to arrange unauthorized access, password theft or attacks against other people's systems.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests and other spaces maintained for this project. They also apply when representing the project elsewhere.

If something goes wrong, stop the interaction and contact the repository maintainer through a contact method they list on their GitHub profile. Do not post private incident details publicly. A public issue can ask for a way to contact a maintainer without including those details. Do not send unsolicited messages to other participants about an incident.

Maintainers should respond fairly, protect private information, explain moderation decisions where appropriate and avoid reviewing an incident in which they have a conflict of interest. They may edit or remove harmful content, close a discussion or restrict participation in project spaces. Serious threats or repeated misconduct may lead to removal from project participation.

**বাংলায়:** সবাইকে সম্মান করুন, নতুনদের ধৈর্য ধরে সাহায্য করুন এবং code নিয়ে আলোচনা করুন। ব্যক্তিগত আক্রমণ, হয়রানি, অন্যের তথ্য প্রকাশ বা অনুমতি ছাড়া আক্রমণের আয়োজন গ্রহণযোগ্য নয়। সমস্যা হলে GitHub profile-এ maintainer-এর দেওয়া যোগাযোগপদ্ধতি ব্যবহার করুন; ব্যক্তিগত ঘটনার তথ্য public issue-তে দেবেন না।
